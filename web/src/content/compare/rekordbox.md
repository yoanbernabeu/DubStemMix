**DubStemMix** does the dub version: one tune's stems on six faders of the Akai MIDImix, a tape echo and a spring on sends, the throws ready. Free. **rekordbox** is the library and performance software of Pioneer DJ / AlphaTheta gear, with real-time stems.

*Facts about rekordbox come from [rekordbox.com](https://rekordbox.com/en/plan/) (rekordbox 7.2), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Six faders for the parts**: every stem has its level, mute and three sends: [The board](../../docs/the-board/).
- **Dub sends, shared buses**: a [tape echo, a plate or spring reverb and a phaser](../../docs/effects/): cut the vocal, its echo keeps going.
- **The dub moves**: [throw, drop, hold, crash, pull-up](../../docs/gestures/), one gesture each.
- **Free and open source**: MIT.

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | rekordbox |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a dub console for one tune's stems | DJ library and performance software |
| Stems | up to 6 strips; split built in (4 stems) or your own files | real-time, 3 or 4 stems: vocal, inst, bass, drums |
| Effects on stems | 3 sends per strip: tape echo, plate or spring, phaser | STEM FX on one stem |
| Akai MIDImix | factory mapping, nothing to set up | MIDI LEARN, depending on plan or hardware |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | macOS and Windows |
| Price | **free**, open source (MIT) | Free plan; paid plans from €10/month |

## What rekordbox does well

The standard for preparing a library and playing it on Pioneer DJ / AlphaTheta club gear, with real-time stems and per-stem effects.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to dub one tune at a time on a MIDImix, part by part, for free.
- **Pick rekordbox** to prepare and play DJ sets on Pioneer DJ / AlphaTheta equipment.

Cut your dub versions in DubStemMix, [record them](../../docs/sets/#record-your-version), and bring them into your rekordbox library.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
