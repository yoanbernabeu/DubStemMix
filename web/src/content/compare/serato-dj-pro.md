**DubStemMix** is built for the dub version: one tune, its stems on six faders, a tape echo and a spring on sends, the throws on the buttons of the Akai MIDImix. Free. **Serato DJ Pro** is a leading DJ app, with real-time Stems to remix while you mix tracks.

*Facts about Serato DJ Pro come from [serato.com](https://serato.com/dj/pro/stems) (Serato DJ Pro 4.0), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Six faders for the parts**: drums, bass, skank, keys, horns, vocal from your own multitracks (a split gives four): each part with its own level, mute and sends. [The board](../../docs/the-board/).
- **Dub sends on every stem**: three sends per strip into a [tape echo, a plate or spring reverb and a phaser](../../docs/effects/), on shared buses: tails ring on after the cut.
- **The dub moves**: [throw, drop to the riddim, hold the echo, crash the spring, pull-up](../../docs/gestures/).
- **Free and open source**: MIT.

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | Serato DJ Pro |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a dub console for one tune's stems | a DJ app for mixing tracks |
| Stems | up to 6 strips; split built in (4 stems) or your own files | real-time, 4 stems: vocals, melody, bass, drums |
| Effects on stems | 3 sends per strip: tape echo, plate or spring, phaser | Stems Pad FX (echoes, brakes) triggered from pads |
| Akai MIDImix | factory mapping, nothing to set up | MIDI Assign |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | macOS and Windows |
| Price | **free**, open source (MIT) | $299 once or $11.99/month; Serato DJ Lite free |

## What Serato DJ Pro does well

Club-proven DJing on two or four decks, scratch and DVS, wide hardware support, and Stems that separate any track in real time to remix it on the fly.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to dub one tune at a time, part by part, with sends and throws, for free.
- **Pick Serato DJ Pro** to DJ: mix many tracks, beatmatch, scratch, on club or controller hardware.

Prepare your dub versions in DubStemMix, [record them](../../docs/sets/#record-your-version) as WAV, and play them in your Serato sets.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
