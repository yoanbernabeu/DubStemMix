**DubStemMix** is made for the dub version: one tune's stems on six faders of the Akai MIDImix, a tape echo and a spring on sends, the throws ready. Free. **VirtualDJ** is an all-round DJ app with real-time stems and effects per stem.

*Facts about VirtualDJ come from [virtualdj.com](https://www.virtualdj.com/buy/) (VirtualDJ 2026), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Six faders for the parts**: drums, bass, skank, keys, horns, vocal from your own multitracks (a split gives four): [The board](../../docs/the-board/).
- **Dub sends, shared buses**: [tape echo, plate or spring, phaser](../../docs/effects/), patchable into each other.
- **The dub moves**: [throw, drop, hold, crash, pull-up](../../docs/gestures/).
- **Free and open source**: MIT.

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | VirtualDJ |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a dub console for one tune's stems | an all-round DJ, video and karaoke app |
| Stems | up to 6 strips; split built in (4 stems) or your own files | real-time: vocal, instruments, bass, kick, hi-hat |
| Effects on stems | 3 sends per strip: tape echo, plate or spring, phaser | any effect on one or more stems |
| Akai MIDImix | factory mapping, nothing to set up | controller mapping (the free edition is limited to entry-level controllers) |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | macOS and Windows |
| Price | **free**, open source (MIT) | free for home use; paid plans from €4/month |

## What VirtualDJ does well

A do-everything DJ app: audio, video and karaoke, a huge controller list, real-time stems with effects on any stem, free for home use.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to dub one tune at a time on a MIDImix, part by part, for free.
- **Pick VirtualDJ** to DJ, with video and karaoke, on almost any controller.

Cut your dub versions in DubStemMix, [record them](../../docs/sets/#record-your-version), and play them in VirtualDJ.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
