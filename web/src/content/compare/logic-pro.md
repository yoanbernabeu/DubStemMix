**DubStemMix** is made for playing: a dub version of a tune's stems, live, with your hands on the Akai MIDImix. Free, ready as it opens. **Logic Pro** is Apple's complete studio, with a superb Stem Splitter and Tape Delay.

*Facts about Logic Pro come from [apple.com](https://www.apple.com/logic-pro/) (Logic Pro 12), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Played with your hands**: six stem strips and two effect strips, mirrored on the MIDImix: [The board](../../docs/the-board/).
- **Dub moves built in**: [throw, drop, hold, crash, pull-up](../../docs/gestures/), one gesture each.
- **Effects voiced for dub**: a tape echo that darkens, a spring that crashes, King Tubby's stepped high-pass: [The effects](../../docs/effects/).
- **Made for the night**: setlists, the next tune armed while one plays, nothing that cuts by mistake: [Playing live](../../docs/live/). And free.

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | Logic Pro |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a dub console for one tune's stems | a complete studio |
| Stem separation | drums, bass, instruments, vocals | drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano, other |
| Tape echo | built in, with HOLD, ×2, tape stop | Tape Delay |
| Spring reverb | built in, with CRASH | spring types in Amp Designer; ChromaVerb, Space Designer |
| Phaser | Bi-Phase-style phaser or tape flanger | Phaser |
| Dub gestures | ready | built with controller assignments or automation |
| Akai MIDImix | every control has its job | preset downloaded from Akai for volume, mute, solo and pan; Learn mode for the rest |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | Mac with Apple silicon, Logic Pro for iPad |
| Price | **free**, open source (MIT) | $199.99 once, or Apple Creator Studio |

## What Logic Pro does well

The whole studio: record, produce, mix and master, a huge library of instruments and effects, and a Stem Splitter that also separates guitar and piano. Ideal for a polished dub mix built with automation.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to play dub versions live on a MIDImix, ready as it opens, for free.
- **Pick Logic Pro** to record, produce and finish music, or to craft a dub mix track by track.

Split or export stems in Logic, then play them in DubStemMix. Your Audio Unit plugins work on its buses and inserts.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
