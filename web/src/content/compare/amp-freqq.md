Both are made for dub and both play on the **Akai MIDImix**. **DubStemMix** goes inside the tune: every part on its own fader, to cut a new version of it, for free. **Amp FreQQ** is a sound-system preamp and dub station for finished stereo tracks.

*Facts about Amp FreQQ come from [reggaepreamp.com](https://reggaepreamp.com/amp-freqq-v3/), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Inside the tune**: drums, bass, guitar, vocal… each on its own fader. [Pull the vocal, keep the riddim, throw one snare hit into the echo](../../docs/gestures/): the moves of the dub engineer, not only of the selector.
- **Stems from any song**: no multitrack? The app [splits the song](../../docs/stems/) into drums, bass, instruments and vocals, and prepares a whole set in one go.
- **Free and open source**: no licence, no activation, MIT. Read the code, change it, add your controller.
- **Ready on the MIDImix**: factory mapping, press SEND ALL, play: [Install and plug in](../../docs/install/).

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | Amp FreQQ |
|---|---|---|
| What you play | the stems of one tune, up to 6 strips | stereo tracks on 2 decks, plus 4 audio inputs |
| Stem separation | built in | not part of it |
| Echo | tape delay, tempo sync, Space Echo heads, HOLD, ×2, tape stop | tape echo inspired by the HH Slider, two modes |
| Reverb | plate or spring, with CRASH | reverb |
| Preamp | stepped high-pass, 3-band kills, dubplate | 4-band isolator with kills, graphic and parametric EQ, resonant filter |
| Siren, samples | no | yes: siren with snapshots, 12-slot sample player |
| Akai MIDImix | factory mapping, nothing to set up | official support, overlays, LED feedback, MIDI learn |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | Windows and Mac |
| Price | **free**, open source (MIT) | from £125; £200 with MIDImix overlays; £310 with the controller |

## What Amp FreQQ does well

A complete sound-system preamp in software: isolator kills, EQs, a resonant filter, an HH Slider style echo, a reverb, a siren and a sample player. It takes any source (files, turntable, CDJ, microphone, DJ software), and runs on Windows and Mac.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to build dub versions from the stems of a tune, on an Apple Silicon Mac, for free.
- **Pick Amp FreQQ** to play finished tracks or vinyl through a sound-system preamp, with a siren and samples, or on Windows.

They fit different moments: DubStemMix to cut and [record dub versions](../../docs/sets/#record-your-version) from stems, Amp FreQQ to play finished tracks through its preamp.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
