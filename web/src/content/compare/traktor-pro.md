**DubStemMix** is made for dub: one tune's stems on six faders of the Akai MIDImix, a tape echo and a spring on sends, the throws ready. Free. **Traktor Pro 4** is Native Instruments' DJ software, with stem separation and Stem Decks.

*Facts about Traktor Pro come from [native-instruments.com](https://www.native-instruments.com/en/products/traktor/dj-software/traktor-pro-4/) (Traktor Pro 4), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Six faders for the parts**: drums, bass, skank, keys, horns, vocal from your own multitracks (a split gives four): [The board](../../docs/the-board/).
- **Dub sends, shared buses**: [tape echo, plate or spring, phaser](../../docs/effects/), patchable into each other.
- **The dub moves**: [throw, drop, hold, crash, pull-up](../../docs/gestures/).
- **Free and open source**: MIT.

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | Traktor Pro |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a dub console for one tune's stems | a four-deck DJ app |
| Stems | up to 6 strips; split built in (4 stems) or your own audio files | 4 stems: drums, bass, instruments, vocals; Stem Decks for .stem.mp4 files |
| Effects on stems | 3 sends per strip: tape echo, plate or spring, phaser | volume, filter and effects per stem, 4 FX units |
| Akai MIDImix | factory mapping, nothing to set up | custom mappings |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | macOS and Windows |
| Price | **free**, open source (MIT) | $149 once |

## What Traktor Pro does well

Four-deck club DJing with Stem Decks, Remix Decks and DVS, stem separation powered by iZotope, and deep custom mappings.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to dub one tune at a time on a MIDImix, part by part, for free.
- **Pick Traktor Pro** to DJ on four decks, with remix and stem decks, DVS and your own mappings.

Cut your dub versions in DubStemMix, [record them](../../docs/sets/#record-your-version), and mix them in your Traktor sets.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
