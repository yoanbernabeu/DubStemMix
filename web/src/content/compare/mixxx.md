Both are **free and open source**. **DubStemMix** is a dub console: one tune's stems on six faders of the Akai MIDImix, stem separation built in, a tape echo and a spring on sends. **Mixxx** is the community DJ app, for mixing tracks.

*Facts about Mixxx come from [mixxx.org](https://mixxx.org/download/) (Mixxx 2.5.6; stems in the 2.6 beta), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Stems from any song**: separation built in: drums, bass, instruments and vocals, no other tool needed. [Stems and splitting](../../docs/stems/).
- **Six faders for the parts**: with three dub sends each: [The board](../../docs/the-board/).
- **The dub moves**: [throw, drop, hold, crash, pull-up](../../docs/gestures/).
- **Ready on the MIDImix**: factory mapping, press SEND ALL, play.

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | Mixxx |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a dub console for one tune's stems | a DJ app for mixing tracks |
| Stems | up to 6 strips; split built in or your own audio files | 2.6 beta: plays NI STEM files made with other tools |
| Effects on stems | 3 sends per strip: tape echo, plate or spring, phaser | 2.6 beta: an effect and its amount per stem |
| Akai MIDImix | factory mapping, nothing to set up | custom mappings in JavaScript |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | Windows, macOS, Linux |
| License, price | MIT, free | GPL, free |

## What Mixxx does well

A complete, free DJ app on Windows, macOS and Linux, built by a large community, with scriptable controller mappings and stem decks on the way.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to dub one tune at a time on a MIDImix, with stem separation built in.
- **Pick Mixxx** to DJ for free, on Linux or Windows too.

Two free tools that fit together: cut your dub versions in DubStemMix, [record them](../../docs/sets/#record-your-version), and mix them in Mixxx.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
