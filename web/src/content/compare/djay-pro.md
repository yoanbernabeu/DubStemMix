**DubStemMix** is built for the dub version: one tune's stems on six faders of the Akai MIDImix, a tape echo and a spring on sends, the throws ready. Free. **djay Pro** is Algoriddim's DJ app for every device, with Neural Mix real-time stems.

*Facts about djay Pro come from [algoriddim.com](https://www.algoriddim.com/djay-pro-mac) (djay Pro 5.6), checked in October 2026.*

## Why DubStemMix for a dub version

- **Six faders for the parts**: every stem with its level, mute and three sends: [The board](../../docs/the-board/).
- **Dub sends, shared buses**: [tape echo, plate or spring, phaser](../../docs/effects/): tails ring on after the cut.
- **The dub moves**: [throw, drop, hold, crash, pull-up](../../docs/gestures/).
- **Free and open source**: MIT.

## At a glance

| | DubStemMix | djay Pro |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a dub console for one tune's stems | a DJ app for every device |
| Stems | up to 6 strips; split built in (4 stems) or your own files | Neural Mix, real-time: drums, bass, harmonics, vocals |
| Effects on stems | 3 sends per strip: tape echo, plate or spring, phaser | Neural Mix FX routed to chosen stems |
| Akai MIDImix | factory mapping, nothing to set up | MIDI Learn |
| Platforms | macOS 15+, Apple Silicon | Mac, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Android, and more |
| Price | **free**, open source (MIT) | free download; PRO subscription |

## What djay Pro does well

DJing anywhere, from a phone to a club controller, with streaming services, AI-assisted mixing and Neural Mix stems on every platform.

## Which one for you

- **Pick DubStemMix** to dub one tune at a time on a MIDImix, part by part, for free.
- **Pick djay Pro** to DJ on any device, with streaming and automatic help.

Cut your dub versions in DubStemMix, [record them](../../docs/sets/#record-your-version), and play them in djay.

## Try DubStemMix

Free, one line in the Terminal, on an Apple Silicon Mac:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yoanbernabeu/DubStemMix/main/install.sh | sh
```

Then follow [your first dub, step by step](../../docs/first-dub/): ten moves, from the stems to your recorded version.
